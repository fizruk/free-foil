Description

Everything about MLTT that is generated rather than written.

The scope-safe term type, the signature bifunctor, the pattern synonyms and the conversions to and from the raw (BNFC) syntax all come out of mkFreeFoil and mkFreeFoilConversions. What is written by hand here is only the instances that the generated types need and Template Haskell cannot supply: the GenericK representations, the bifunctor instances of the signature, and the pattern instances of Pattern'.

Convert back to raw syntax, naming free and bound variables separately.

A variable free in the whole term is named from a NameMap; a bound one is named from its index. Keeping the two apart matters because raw names are not unique across scope indices: a definition's body is elaborated before the definition's own name is allocated, so def f := λ x ⇒ x gives both f and the x it binds the raw name 0.
